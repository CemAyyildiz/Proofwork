# Customer development records

What Proofwork learned from talking to people is kept here as written records: one per team interview, one summary of the Month 1 contributors' feedback, and one for the debrief with the team that ran the pilot campaign. All three use the same format, below, so they can be read side by side.

This folder holds the format and, once they are written, the records. No record is in it yet: nothing here is filled in until the conversation it describes has taken place.

## Records to write

| Record | How many | File name | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team interview | at least five | `interview-<nn>-<team>.md`, for example `interview-01-<team>.md` | open · Cem |
| Month 1 contributor feedback, one summary of all answers | one | `contributor-feedback.md` | open · Cem |
| Pilot debrief | one | `debrief-<team>.md` | open · Cem |

When a record is added, link it from the [Month 2 evidence index](../month-2.md) and change its row here.

## Rules

- **Teams are named, people are not.** A record gives the person's role and nothing that identifies or reaches them: no name, no email, phone number, handle, wallet address or link to a profile.
- **Consent comes first.** Every record says whether the team agreed to be named and whether it agreed to be quoted, and when it said so. This repository is public.
  - Not agreed to be named: use a neutral label (`Team A`, `Team B`) in the text and in the file name, and leave out anything that would give the team away.
  - Not agreed to be quoted: write what was said in your own words and leave the Quotes section as "none, not agreed".
- **Only what was said.** A record describes a conversation that happened. No answer is filled in from memory of another conversation, guessed, or tidied into something the person did not say. A question that was not asked or not answered is written as "not asked" or "not answered". The writer's own conclusions do not go in a record.
- **Quotes are exact or absent.** Words between quotation marks are the person's own, in the language they used, with a translation after them if that helps. A paraphrase is not put in quotation marks.
- **A recording is linked, not described.** If the conversation was recorded and the team agreed, the link goes in the record's first table. No link is written before the recording exists.
- **Contributor feedback is one summary.** It counts answers and does not single out a contributor: no wallet, no handle, no submission ID.

## The format

Copy everything inside the block into a new file and fill it in. Keep every heading, also when its answer is "not asked".

```markdown
# <Kind>: <team or label>

| | |
|---|---|
| Kind | interview · contributor feedback · debrief (keep one) |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Team | <team name, or a neutral label when the team did not agree to be named> |
| Role of the person | <for example: community lead, founder, developer relations. No name> |
| How it was held | <call, chat, in person, form> · <length, or number of answers> |
| Agreed to be named | yes · no, on YYYY-MM-DD |
| Agreed to be quoted | yes · no, on YYYY-MM-DD |
| Recording | <link, when one exists and the team agreed> · none |

## What they do today

## What goes wrong

## What they check before paying

## What they would change, and what they would pay for

## Quotes
```

## What goes under each heading

The headings are the same for the three kinds of record. What they ask differs a little:

| Heading | Team interview | Contributor feedback | Pilot debrief |
|---|---|---|---|
| What they do today | How the team runs paid campaigns or bounties now: where, how often, how big, who decides, how it pays | How contributors found the campaign and what they did to take part, step by step | How the team ran its campaign on Proofwork: what it set up, how long each step took, who did it |
| What goes wrong | What farming and fake submissions look like for them, what it costs, what they tried against it | Where contributors got stuck or gave up: wallet, trustline, connecting X, submitting, the decision, the re-review | What got in the team's way: requesting access, the readiness check, funding, approving, releasing, closing, getting the remainder back |
| What they check before paying | What the team looks at before it pays someone, and how long that takes per submission | Whether contributors understood why they were approved or rejected, and whether they opened the reason or the verify page | What the team looked at before it released each payout, and whether the review and its reasons were enough for it |
| What they would change, and what they would pay for | What they would want from a tool for this, and whether and how they pay for anything like it today | What contributors would change about taking part | What the team would change, and what it would pay for |
| Quotes | Their own words, when they agreed | The same, with no way to tell who said it | The same |

For the contributor feedback record, "Team" is the group that was asked ("Month 1 contributors"), "Role of the person" is "contributor", and "How it was held" gives the number of answers out of the number of people asked.
