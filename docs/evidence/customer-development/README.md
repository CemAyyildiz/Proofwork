## Stellar Türkiye chapter

Asked where Proofwork stands and whether to apply to the Stellar Community Fund now. Advice: apply in a later round, after the third Instawards month, once Proofwork has some integrations and users.

## Pilot campaign

No team ran a pilot campaign on testnet in Month 2. Teams fund campaigns to reach real users and pay real rewards, which testnet cannot give them, so a testnet pilot would not show whether a team would use Proofwork. Three to five campaigns by teams are planned for Month 3 on mainnet. Until then, the path a team takes, from requesting funder access to getting its remainder back, is shown by the [external-funder rehearsal](../external-funder-rehearsal.md), run with a wallet that never had a role.
